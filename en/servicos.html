Automation and remote support

Remote access to machines

Remote access to packaging machines enables real-time technical diagnostics without immediate field travel, reducing downtime and improving OEE in industrial automation environments.

R.R Packaging Parts structures remote technical support with a secure, encrypted architecture. Connection is established via an industrial modem linked to the machine’s industrial PLC or directly to the PLC when the equipment already supports remote access. Through this link, our packaging automation specialists access the machine interface and main controllers of the production cell.

This infrastructure allows us to work on lines from different packaging machine manufacturers, performing remote machine diagnostics, checking I/O states, analyzing alarms and evaluating communication between PLCs, HMIs, drives and other field devices.

Benefits of remote access

  • Major downtime reduction. Fault identification and resolution in minutes, avoiding long stops while waiting for a technician to reach the plant.
  • Operational savings. Lower costs for travel, lodging and unproductive specialist travel time for remote industrial maintenance.
  • Data-driven decisions. Real-time visibility of production performance, process parameters and alarms, enabling planned or immediate strategic adjustments.
  • Data security. Advanced authentication and end-to-end encryption, with practices aligned with privacy regulations, ensuring only authorized users access the machine system.

Reverse Engineering

Every supplied part goes through improvement and functionality studies.

We perform technical analysis of existing parts and assemblies to understand their geometry, operation and performance in real conditions.

From this assessment, we develop the component's technical model and evaluate aspects such as materials, manufacturing processes and actual usage conditions. Based on this information, we identify improvement opportunities aimed at increasing durability, reliability and overall system efficiency.

More than reproducing, our focus is to evolve each part, correcting potential limitations of the original design and adapting the component to process requirements.
